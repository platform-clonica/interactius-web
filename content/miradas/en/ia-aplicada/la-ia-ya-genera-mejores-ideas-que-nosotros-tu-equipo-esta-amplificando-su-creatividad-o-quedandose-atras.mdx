During centuries, we believed creativity was our inviolable refuge — the purest and most exclusively human corner of the mind. But the evidence no longer leaves room for doubt: **AI outperforms humans in divergent creativity, generating more ideas and more original ones**.

This was demonstrated by a recent study published in _Scientific Reports_ in 2024, in which ChatGPT-4 was compared with 151 human participants across three divergent thinking tests. The verdict was clear: **AI did not merely match human creative capacity — it surpassed it**.

Striking? Yes. Unsettling? Yes.
